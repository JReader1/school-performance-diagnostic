# Illustrative administrator decision brief

**Caelora Academy | Reader Analytics | Synthetic data only**

## Executive summary
A fictional private K–12 school is exploring enrollment patterns and stakeholder experience. The appropriate objective is to identify **questions to investigate**, not establish a causal explanation.

## Example findings
- Year-end enrollment rises from **392** (2023–24) to **407** (2024–25) to **423** (2025–26). Enrollment levels alone cannot distinguish retention, new recruitment and graduating cohorts.
- Generated parent respondents report mean overall experience scores of approximately **3.77**, **3.82**, and **3.93 out of 5** over the three school years. Those are unmatched and potentially selective response samples.
- The dashboard separately exposes Academic Growth, Character & Culture, and Family Partnership as **fictional** survey topics. Their scores do not establish actual institutional quality.
- Academic benchmark aggregates are synthetic and do not measure actual standards or individual educational progress.

## What a real engagement would require
1. Confirm the school's actual pillars and one decision school leadership needs to make.
2. Review governance, parent/student consent, data access, privacy, and safe aggregation.
3. Co-design and pilot survey items, validate population frames and measure nonresponse.
4. Reconcile annual enrollment and admissions records before reporting retention.
5. Interpret findings with uncertainty and limitations, then propose measured interventions.

**No real-school claims are authorized by this demonstration.**
