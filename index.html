01 / interpretation

Signals worth investigating

Indicators are descriptive. Small groups, changing cohorts, response bias, nonresponse, and the data-generation assumptions restrict interpretation.

02 / proposed engagement

From evidence to an administrative decision

A real engagement would begin by confirming the school's actual mission pillars, reviewing existing data and consent requirements, defining survey eligibility, and agreeing on which decisions leadership wants to support. Only then would Reader Analytics develop appropriate measurements and recommendations.
